A place to learn,
build and belong.

BSK ICT Club is a student technology community at Blessed Sacrament Secondary School Kimaanya, Masaka City. We are growing a culture of curiosity, collaboration and practical learning.

Our direction

Technology is better
when it is shared.

The Blessed Sacrament Kimaanya Secondary School ICT Club was started in 2022 by Tumwine Kelly, with a dream of promoting earlier exposure of students to advanced technology skills.

The club aims to promote ICT among students in areas of artificial intelligence, robotics, cloud computing, embedded systems and other emerging technologies.

Guided by our motto, “Levereging ICT to spur Innovations”, we learn through peer teaching, experiments and projects, and make space for students to explore technology together.
